Getting in

Join Full Lab Link

  1. Press Join Full Lab, on the site or in #unlock-full-access on Discord.
  2. Pay by card on Whop. The first 20 members can use the code FOUNDING40 for €29 per month instead of €69.
  3. After paying, Whop opens a Discord page. Press Connect Account and pick the Discord account you use in the EditLab server.
  4. Your Full Lab Member role is added, the Full Lab channels appear, and the courses open in your Whop account.

Your role didn't show up Link

  1. Make sure you joined the EditLab Discord with the account you want to use.
  2. On whop.com, open EditLab Academy, then Discord, and press Connect Account again.
  3. Pick the same Discord account that is in the server. The role is added within a minute.

Still nothing after a few minutes? Open a ticket in #support and choose Payment or access.

Using Full Lab

Watch the courses Link

  1. Open Courses in EditLab Academy on Whop, signed in with the email you paid with.
  2. Pick a course. Lessons are in order, and project files are attached under each lesson.
  3. On Discord, press Follow on a course's post in #full-course. You get a notification each time a new lesson is out.

There are seven courses: Basics, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Motion Graphics, 3D (Blender), Getting Clients, and Pricing and Branding. Lessons are released as each one is finished.

Get feedback on an edit Link

  1. In #pro-feedback, open a new post. One post per edit.
  2. Title it with what the video is, and pick the tag for the software you used.
  3. Add the link (YouTube unlisted, Google Drive or Frame.io), what it's for, and what you want notes on: hook, pacing, sound, color or captions.
  4. Susano replies inside your post. Post the new cut in the same post when you've applied the notes.

Use Client Hunter Link

#client-hunter posts paid editing jobs from Reddit, YTJobs and six remote job boards, checked every hour. Editors advertising themselves and unpaid requests are filtered out.

  • Budget is what the client wrote. A 🔥 means a high budget for that kind of work.
  • Work is the kind of edit: short-form, long-form, gaming, podcast and so on.
  • Posted tells you how fresh the job is. Clients get the most replies in the first hours.
  • Competition: Low appears when YTJobs marks a job as low competition.

Read the Editing DM Guide before you reply, then apply on the original post with View on….

Billing

Find your renewal date Link

  1. Sign in to whop.com with the email you joined Full Lab with.
  2. Open your profile, then Orders. In the Whop app, tap your profile picture, then Manage Orders.
  3. Your EditLab Full Lab subscription shows when it renews next.

Cancel your subscription Link

  1. On whop.com, open your profile, then Orders. In the app, tap your profile picture, then Manage Orders.
  2. Select EditLab Full Lab and choose the option to cancel.

You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you aren't charged again.

The founding price stays only while your subscription is active. If it ends and you come back later, the regular price applies.

A payment failed Link

  1. Open Orders on whop.com and update the card on your EditLab Full Lab subscription.
  2. Whop retries a failed payment for up to five days, and you keep access while it does.
  3. If no payment goes through by then, the subscription ends and the Full Lab role is removed. Join again any time.

Refunds Link

  • In the EU, the UK and other countries with a withdrawal right, you can withdraw from your first purchase within 14 days. Where the law allows, days you already had access may be deducted.
  • Charged twice, charged in error, or locked out by a problem on our side? We refund that payment.
  • To ask for one, open a ticket in #support and choose Payment or access. Full Lab access ends when the refund is completed.

Support

Open a support ticket Link

  1. Go to #support on the EditLab Discord.
  2. Press the button that fits: Payment or access, Course question, or Something else.
  3. Fill in the short form. A private channel opens that only you and the EditLab team can see, and the team replies there.

Never post card details, passwords or login codes, in a ticket or anywhere else.

Nothing here matches that. Open a ticket in #support on the Discord and the team will answer.

Still stuck?

Open a ticket in #support. Only you and the EditLab team see it.
