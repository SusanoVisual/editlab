How you learn here

Watching tutorials doesn't make an editor. Finishing edits and having them reviewed does, so every lesson is built around that loop.

  1. WatchOne lesson, one skill.
  2. PracticeUse the project files to repeat it yourself.
  3. SubmitPost your edit in the feedback channel.
  4. Get feedbackNotes on what works and what to fix.
  5. ImproveApply the notes and post the new cut.

How joining Full Lab works

Payment runs through Whop. Your Discord access is added automatically.

  1. Pay on Whop. Press Join Full Lab and pay by card. Founding members enter FOUNDING40.
  2. Connect Discord. After paying, Whop shows a Discord page. Press Connect Account and pick your Discord account.
  3. Open Full Lab. Your Full Lab Member role is added and the private channels appear in the EditLab server.

Cancel anytime in your Whop account. Access runs until the end of the period you paid for. A question or a problem with payment? Message Susano on Discord.

Is it for you?

Better to know before you join.

A good fit if you

  • Want to earn from editing, not only learn effects
  • Are starting out, or stuck without steady clients
  • Will post your work and act on the feedback
  • Are fine joining early, while the courses are still being made

Not a fit if you

  • Want a finished course library today
  • Expect clients or income to be guaranteed
  • Only want free plugins and nothing else

Questions

Are the courses finished?

No. All five are in production, and lessons are released as each one is done. That is why the first 20 members get the founding price.

What do I get today if I become a Full Lab member?

Feedback on your edits from Susano, the Client Hunter feed, and every lesson from the day it is released.

Do I need experience?

No. Join free, look around and ask questions. The courses are planned to start from an empty project.

Will Client Hunter get me a job?

It shows you paid job posts in one place. It can't promise how many appear or that you get hired. That part is your message and your work.

How do I pay for Full Lab?

Press Join Full Lab and pay by card on Whop. Then press Connect Account on the Discord page Whop shows you, and the Full Lab channels unlock. If anything goes wrong, message Susano on Discord.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your Whop account. Your access runs until the end of the period you paid for.

What language is it in?

English. The server, the lessons and the guide are all in English.
